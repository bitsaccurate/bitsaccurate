## 👋 Who We Are

**BitsAccurate** is a software development studio based in **Delhi, India** — specializing in cross-platform mobile applications and modern web development. We partner with startups and growing businesses across **India and the Middle East/Gulf region** to turn ideas into polished, production-ready digital products.

We don't just write code. We take full ownership — from architecture and design to deployment and beyond.
